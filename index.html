I live in Columbus, Ohio, and study electrical and computer engineering at The Ohio State University.

This past summer I was an electrical test engineer intern at NASA Glenn Research Center, through HX5, on thermal-vacuum facility electrical systems. I was also electrical lead on a five-intern solid rocket motor test stand that measured thrust at 80 Hz and reproduced published impulse curves to 0.1%.

Most of what I build sits on the line between hardware and software. I built a model rocket and ESP32 flight computer that flew to 390.7 ft and matched its simulated apogee to 0.2%, a neural network inference accelerator that runs an ASL hand-sign classifier on FPGA fabric at 100 MHz with no CPU, and a barbell velocity tracker whose data never leaves the device.

Before Ohio State I studied engineering at Lorain County Community College. Outside of school it is AI/ML, Bitcoin, space, and a long list of books.
